[Reference Image]: Mercury_shot.png

[Voiceover Narration (1:15 - 2:15)]:
"Our first stop brings us to Mercury, the smallest and innermost planet. Mercury is a world of extreme contradictions. With virtually no atmosphere to trap heat or shield its crust, temperatures here swing wildly from an incinerating four hundred and thirty degrees Celsius during the day, down to a freezing minus one hundred and eighty degrees Celsius at night. Its surface resembles our Moon, scarred by billions of years of meteorite impacts. Despite being so close to the Sun, radar observations reveal water ice hiding permanently inside deep, shadowy polar craters where sunlight never reaches. Mercury orbits the Sun at an astonishing speed of forty-seven kilometers per second, completing an entire year in just eighty-eight Earth days."

[Voiceover Narration (2:15 - 3:15)]:
"Moving outward, we arrive at Earth’s so-called twin in size, Venus. But beneath its radiant, golden-hazed cloud canopy lies a living inferno. Venus suffers from a catastrophic runaway greenhouse effect. Its atmosphere is over ninety-six percent carbon dioxide, creating a crushing atmospheric pressure ninety times greater than that of Earth—equivalent to being one kilometer beneath the ocean. Surface temperatures soar past four hundred and sixty degrees Celsius, hot enough to melt lead. Strangely, Venus spins backwards compared to most planets, in retrograde motion, meaning on Venus, the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east. It is a stark reminder of how fragile a planet's climate balance truly is."
